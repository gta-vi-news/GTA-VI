Grand Theft Auto VI Overview

Important information about the next Grand Theft Auto experience.

Welcome to Leonida

Grand Theft Auto VI is an upcoming open-world action-adventure game developed and published by Rockstar Games. The story takes place in the fictional state of Leonida, including a return to the iconic Vice City.


The story follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. After an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves caught in a criminal conspiracy stretching across Leonida. Their story explores trust, loyalty and survival.


Rockstar's official materials describe GTA VI as a single-player experience.

Main Characters

Get to know the two central characters of GTA VI.

MAIN CHARACTER

Jason Duval

Jason grew up around people involved in scams and crime. After spending time in the Army, he found himself in the Keys working for local criminals. His meeting with Lucia may change the direction of his life.

MAIN CHARACTER

Lucia Caminos

Lucia learned to fight from an early age. Her life has been full of challenges, and her time in Leonida Penitentiary has shaped her outlook. She is determined to make smarter choices for her future.
